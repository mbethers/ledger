Educational decision-support tool only — not investment, financial, legal, or tax advice. Formulas are simplified teaching versions of Graham, Buffett, and Lynch's methods and are assumption-sensitive, especially the DCF, Graham Revised, and PEG. Live data comes from free public sources and can lag, mismatch fiscal periods, or fail — verify every figure against a primary source before acting, and see the Inputs & Sources tab for what fed each number.

Data path: SEC EDGAR and Yahoo Finance block direct browser requests (no CORS headers), so those two are requested through the free, keyless r.jina.ai reader relay. That relay sees which tickers you look up. US Treasury yields are fetched directly from treasury.gov. No API keys, accounts, or backend are required; optional keys you add under “Data keys” go directly to Alpha Vantage / Twelve Data, never through the relay.
